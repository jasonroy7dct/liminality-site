Road to FIRE

A Chrome Extension designed to suppress impulse spending by converting e-commerce prices into ETF share equivalents (VOO, SPY, 0050) on-the-fly.

01 / Price Interception Engine
02 / Multi-Market Resolution
03 / Compound Growth Projection
Install Chrome Extension ↗
chrome-extension://road-to-fire/dashboard.html
Interface

Visualizing Saved Capital

The extension intercepts purchase paths on major e-commerce platforms. Instead of seeing a checkout price, it displays how many hours of life or ETF index fractions you are trading away, prompting deliberate financial decisions.

Analytics

Personal FIRE Dashboard

Track avoided expenditures inside your browser. Every suppressed impulse purchase is logged and compiled into a localized wealth growth projection chart, directly mapping your distance to financial freedom.

System Specifications

Technical breakdown & runtime parameters.

Architecture Manifest V3 Chrome Extension, local-first engine
Price Resolution Synchronous price-parsing algorithm with regional locale formatting
Supported Indices VOO (Vanguard S&P 500), SPY, QQQ, TSM, 0050 (元大台灣50), 2330
Data Privacy Zero external database dependency. 100% Client-side sandbox isolation.
Dependencies Pure Javascript vanilla implementation. Zero remote execution scripts.
